\begin{abstract}
We study a solver architecture for real linear equality systems
\(Ax=b\), \(A\in\mathbb{R}^{m\times n}\), \(b\in\mathbb{R}^m\), without
assuming consistency, full rank, or a unique solution. The state is an affine
bundle in canonical coordinates \((P,x)\), where \(P\) projects onto the
accepted normal space and \(x\) is the minimum-norm point satisfying the
accepted constraints. For an incoming equality \(a^Tz=\beta\), the quantities
\(g=(I-P)a\) and \(\rho=\beta-a^Tx\) give an exact insertion calculus:
nonzero \(g\) grows the bundle by one normal direction, while zero \(g\)
separates redundancy from contradiction.

In finite precision the fast router reports numerical status, epistemic
strength, a supported numerical-rank interval, and fixed-solution quality
separately, and leaves rows near a rank transition unresolved rather than
forcing a binary decision. Compressed rows are proposal data, not source-row
evidence: every route that can raise the deterministic source-rank lower
bound carries a formation-error provenance budget and must pass an
a-posteriori source-rank certificate.

Certification is path independent. Three compact proof-object types target
exact uniqueness, exact consistency with nonuniqueness, and exact
inconsistency, and a separate strict-arithmetic checker derives, rather than trusts, an
outward bound \(\eta_S\) in a rowwise augmented relative
perturbation metric; acceptance implies a nearby real system of that exact
type within \(\eta_S\). The audit API attempts all three types independently
of the router and returns a router-independent profile
\(\boldsymbol{\eta}=(\eta_{\rm uniq},\eta_{\rm inf},\eta_{\rm inc})\). These
are existence upper bounds, not confidence scores: the exact status sets are
not topologically separated, and at a compatible rank-deficient point all
three distances can vanish together.

Two property batteries, of 8{,}335 and 2{,}271 checks, report no hard
failure. The experiments support a restricted claim of practical viability
for dense equality classification, not universal superiority over
general-purpose numerical linear algebra.
\end{abstract}
